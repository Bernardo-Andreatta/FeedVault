## Full description (max 4000 characters)

FeedVault is a private, local-only media gallery for Android. No cloud,
no accounts, no data collection — your photos and videos never leave
your device.

ORGANIZE IT YOUR WAY
• Tag any photo or video, and label the people in it
• Filter the whole library instantly by tag, person or favorites
• Switch between a scrollable feed and a grid
• Sort by date, name, or shuffle

VIDEO CLIPS
Trim and save named segments from long videos, with their own tags.
Open a clip and it plays back from the right moment automatically.

FULLSCREEN PLAYBACK
Swipe through media fullscreen with double-tap zoom, seek gestures, and
per-video resume — pick up exactly where you left off.

THE VAULT — YOUR PRIVATE CORNER OF THE GALLERY
Move sensitive photos and videos into the app's own private storage,
locked behind your password or biometrics. Once in the Vault, a file
disappears from the system gallery, from other apps, and from USB file
transfer — but still plays back instantly, no decryption step, because
it never left the app's private space. Restore anytime and it goes
straight back to its original folder.

IMPORT FROM ANYWHERE
Pick a whole folder or individual files through Android's own file
picker. Nothing is uploaded — everything is indexed locally on your
device.

YOUR DATA, PORTABLE
Export every tag, person label and clip as a single JSON file. It's
your backup, on your terms.

PRIVACY AS ARCHITECTURE, NOT A PROMISE
FeedVault has no server, no account system, and no advertising or
analytics SDKs. No data is ever collected or transmitted — the app
works entirely offline from the very first launch. The project is open
source, so anyone can verify exactly what runs on their phone.

Built for anyone with hundreds — or thousands — of photos and videos
who wants a gallery that's organized, fast, and answers to no one but
them.
